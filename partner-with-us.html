About our programs

Ways we show up

+

Weekly Runs

Our weekly morning runs are the backbone of Coffee Club. This is what provides women a place to get in their weekly miles to stay consistent and accountable in the morning because miles are easier with a friend than by yourself. These are the runs that provide a place for relationships to grow and women to foster friendships.

+

Training Programs

We build seasonal training programs where women pursue big goals and do hard things together. With structured support, shared race journeys, and built-in accountability, our programs make long-distance running more fun. At Coffee Club, no one trains alone.

+

Community Events + Experiences

From strength sessions and workshops to themed runs, our events create opportunities for movement outside of running. They bring the community together in new ways to move, learn, and connect.

Long-term Partnerships

Support Coffee Club long term and your brand becomes part of our routine, milestones, and shared experiences that keep women showing up for themselves and each other. Your support helps our community whether they are training for their first race, chasing a new goal, or discovering what is possible. Together, we can create authentic experiences that foster connection and keep women running and training longer and more consistently.

Community Events

Supporting our community events allows us to continue offering accessible, community-driven experiences that encourage women to try new things, build confidence, and form lasting connections. For brand partners, it is an opportunity to engage directly with an invested community authentically, and aligned with women runners.
