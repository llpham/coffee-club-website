About our programs

Ways we show up

+

Weekly Runs

Our weekly morning runs are the heartbeat of Coffee Club. These consistent meetups create routine, accountability, and connection — helping women show up for themselves before real life begins. With recurring runs across our communities, we create a space where training feels more sustainable and friendships are built mile by mile.

+

Training Programs

We’re building seasonal training experiences designed to help women go after big goals together. Through structured support, shared race journeys, and accountability, our training programs make long-distance running feel more approachable — and remind women they do not have to train alone.

+

Community Events + Experiences

From strength sessions and educational workshops to themed runs and community experiences, our events create moments beyond the miles. These one-off experiences bring women together in new ways, introduce fresh ways to move, and keep the journey fun, inspiring, and sustainable.
