About Coffee Club

Run. Sip. Repeat.

Coffee Club started the way most great things do: with a few friends, some early alarms, and a shared love for movement and morning coffee.

Back then, we met at 5AM. Before the sun. Before the city woke up. We ran quiet miles side by side, swapped stories, and always ended with a coffee in hand. What began as a small circle grew into a community of runners, new moms, dog lovers, and people looking for consistency, connection, and joy.

Now, Coffee Club is a women-centered, all-are-welcome run club built around early miles, strong coffee, and even stronger friendships.

Whether you are getting back into running, training for something, or simply looking for friends to move with in the morning light, we would love to meet you. You do not need to be fast. You just need to be up for showing up.

FAQ

Questions before your first run

Is this women only or are men allowed?

Coffee Club is a women-centered, inclusive running community, and everyone is welcome on our runs as long as they respect the space we have built for women. Keep the vibe supportive, do not take over the pace or conversation, and help make it comfortable for everyone. Our group chat is for women only, but all are welcome to show up, run, and grab coffee with us.

Do you only run on Fridays?

Our main group run happens every Friday, plus we host a monthly weekend run that rotates locations. The rest of the week, runners often coordinate their own meetups through our women-only group chat.

What if I am a beginner? Can I still come?

Everyone is welcome. We usually run 8:30-9:30 pace, sometimes 10:30, but it is no big deal if you need to slow down, walk, or just come for coffee. The best pace is the one that works for you.

What if I want more miles?

Come at 5AM for an additional 3 miles and be back by 5:30AM for the main run.

Do you start right away?

We give everyone about a 4-minute grace period to park and get settled before we start.

How do I find updates about meetups?

We post all our official run details on Instagram. For unofficial, member-coordinated runs during the week, we use our women-only group chat. If you would like to join the chat, just ask one of us at a run.

It seems like everyone is good friends

We get it. That can be intimidating. Most of us met through Coffee Club. We will welcome you in, but do not be afraid to say hi, introduce yourself, and join the conversation. The more you show up and put yourself out there, the more this will feel like your crew too.

Is it free to join?

Yep. No fees, no sign-ups, no strings. Just show up, run, and grab coffee after if you would like. The only thing we ask is that you respect the space and the people who make it special.

How do I get connected or find someone to run with?

If you want a running buddy or have any questions before your first run, just DM us. We are happy to connect you with someone and answer anything you are wondering.
